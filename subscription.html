Subscription Policy

Plans

Basic ৳1,500 monthly / ৳15,000 yearly. Pro ৳2,500 monthly / ৳25,000 yearly. No free plan or trial. Feature inclusion must be confirmed before purchase.

Storage and SMS

Default storage 300 MB. Additional 1 GB is ৳2,000 as a stated one-time permanent add-on, subject to allocation. SMS is ৳0.39 each and billed separately.

Payment and renewal

Stated methods: bKash, Nagad and bank transfer. Activation follows payment verification. Renewal requires payment confirmation. No automatic debit is promised unless a valid recurring payment mandate is separately agreed.

Expiry and cancellation

After expiry, app login/access may be blocked until renewal. No guaranteed grace period is specified. Cancellation and refund are governed by their separate policies.

Data export

Schools can export available records and should do so before expiry. Contact dolpiinshelp@gmail.com for written plan confirmation.
