ABOUT

Technology for everyday school operations.

Schoobi helps schools organise student information, staff records, attendance, fees, examinations, communication and reporting.

Purpose

School teams manage connected responsibilities. Schoobi aims to make these workflows easier for authorised users.

Product

Schoobi User is the school-facing application. Schools manage their own records and decide which authorised staff may access them. Students can view selected public information through school-issued QR codes.
