Cancellation Policy

Request cancellation

Email dolpiinshelp@gmail.com or call +8801632426210. Include school name, registered contact, plan and requested cancellation date. Authority may be verified.

Effective date

Support will confirm the end date and whether a renewal payment has already been processed. Cancellation does not reverse a completed payment automatically.

Renewal

Request cancellation before the next renewal period. Automatic debit is not promised unless a valid mandate has separately been agreed. A separate payment provider arrangement may need cancellation with that provider.

After cancellation

Access may continue until paid expiry. After expiry, login may be blocked until a new subscription is confirmed. Export needed school records before expiry.

Refund

Cancellation alone does not create a refund entitlement. See Refund Policy.
