# Google Play publication checklist
This is a verification checklist, not a claim of approval or compliance.
- [ ] Publish a public privacy policy and link it in the app and Play Console.
- [ ] Audit actual app and SDK collection/sharing; complete Data safety accurately.
- [ ] If users create accounts, implement the required in-app deletion pathway and maintain this external request route.
- [ ] Implement actual backend deletion; this page only prepares an email request.
- [ ] Separate account deletion from school-controlled student record requests.
- [ ] Verify target-age groups, Families Policy, child-directed SDKs and parental/school authorisation.
- [ ] Verify Google Play Billing requirements for digital subscriptions and any applicable regional exception.
- [ ] Ensure subscription price, billing period, renewal, cancellation and refund disclosures match the app.
- [ ] Verify package name, app links and policy URLs in Play Console.
- [ ] Obtain legal review of Bangladesh consumer, privacy and subscription terms.
