Refund Policy

General rule: subscription payments are generally non-refundable after purchase.

Technical fault review

If a verified technical fault attributable to Schoobi materially prevents use of a paid service, a partial refund may be considered after investigation. A possible range is 30%–60% of the relevant subscription payment depending on evidence, duration and impact. This is discretionary, negotiable and not guaranteed. No 100% refund is promised.

Request review

Email dolpiinshelp@gmail.com with school name, registered contact, plan, payment date, transaction reference, issue description and evidence. Never send passwords or access tokens.

Assessment

Schoobi may verify account, payment and fault, then communicate its decision through official contact. Change of mind, unused time, user device/network issues and incorrect school data are not normally eligible, subject to mandatory law.

Legal rights

This policy does not remove consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under Bangladesh law.
