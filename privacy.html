Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how Schoobi, operated by Muktadir Shabab in Bangladesh, handles information connected with Schoobi User and school QR profiles.

Information

Depending on features used, information may include names, email, phone, school details, student identifiers, photos, class, roll, attendance, fees, payment records, results, teacher details, messages, documents and account information. Technical logs or device information may be processed where required. This is a list of possible categories, not a claim that every category is collected from every user.

Purpose and school control

Information supports school workflows, accounts, reports, communications, security and support. Schools control records they enter, decide authorised access and must provide required notices and permissions. Schoobi processes records to provide the service.

QR profiles

Anyone scanning a public QR may see its selected information. Schools should not publish private fees, confidential results, credentials or restricted records in public QR profiles.

Providers and sharing

Google, Cloudflare and Cloudinary may act as service or infrastructure providers where applicable. They may process data to provide their services. Schoobi does not sell student or school personal information. Information may be disclosed to relevant providers, on school instructions, with permission or when legally required.

Security

HTTPS is used in transit. Access is intended to be restricted to authorised accounts and permitted school data. No online service guarantees absolute security. Users must protect credentials; schools should remove unnecessary access.

Children

School data may concern children under 13. Schools must ensure lawful authority, appropriate parent/guardian notices and required permissions. Data should be limited to the school purpose. No COPPA, GDPR or other certification is claimed.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the school, legal requirements and operational needs. Account deletion requests go to dolpiinshelp@gmail.com. Student academic, attendance and fee records are school-controlled; students/guardians should contact the principal. Identity and authority verification may be required. Legal, dispute and backup retention may apply.

International processing

Providers may process information outside Bangladesh, subject to applicable requirements and provider safeguards.
